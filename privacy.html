Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

This policy explains what information Start Systems collects when you use our automation tools, how it is used, and what choices you have. If you have questions about anything here, email hello@startsystems.dev.

Who we are

Start Systems builds and operates workflow automations for real estate professionals. Some of those automations connect to services you already use, such as Google and Facebook Messenger, with your permission.

Information we access

Our automations only access the accounts you explicitly connect, and only the data needed to perform the task you have asked for. Depending on which automation you use, this may include:

  • Google Calendar — reading, creating, updating, and deleting calendar events on calendars you have authorized.
  • Google Tasks — reading, creating, updating, and deleting tasks on task lists you have authorized.
  • Gmail and Google Drive — where an automation you have set up requires reading messages or documents in order to produce a summary or briefing.
  • Messages you send to our assistants — the text of instructions you send through Facebook Messenger or a similar channel, so the request can be understood and carried out.

We do not request access to accounts or data types beyond what a given automation needs to function.

How we use it

Information is used for one purpose: to carry out the automation you have configured. For example, a message asking to schedule an appointment is used to create that calendar event and a matching task.

To interpret instructions written in plain language, message content may be sent to a third-party AI service (currently Google's Gemini models) for processing. Only the content needed to understand and complete the request is sent.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers required to operate the automation itself.

Google user data

Start Systems' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, Google user data is used only to provide or improve the features you have asked for, is never transferred to others except as needed to provide those features or as required by law, is never used for advertising, and is never read by a human except with your explicit permission, for security purposes, or where required by law.

Storage and retention

Our automations are designed to act on information rather than accumulate it. Data retrieved from a connected account is processed to complete the task and is not retained beyond what is needed for the automation to run. Execution logs that may contain request details are automatically deleted after a limited period.

Credentials that authorize access to your accounts are stored in encrypted form on our own infrastructure and are used solely to perform the automations you have set up.

Your choices

You can revoke our access to your Google account at any time from your Google account permissions page. Doing so immediately stops any automation that relies on that connection.

You may also contact us to ask what information is held in connection with your automations, or to request that it be deleted.

Children

Our services are intended for business use and are not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date above.

Contact

Questions or requests can be sent to hello@startsystems.dev.
